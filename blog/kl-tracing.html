Taming Generative Video Models for Zero-Shot Optical Flow Extraction

Seungwoo Kim, Khai Loong Aw, Klemen Kotar, Cristobal Eyzaguirre, Wanhee Lee, Yunong Liu, Jared Watrous, Stefan Stojanov, Juan Carlos Niebles, Jiajun Wu, Daniel L. K. Yamins.

Can a pre-trained video model give us optical flow without task-specific training? We show yes — with a test-time trick we call KL-tracing.

The idea

Inject a localized perturbation into the first frame, roll the model forward one step, and compute the Kullback–Leibler divergence between the perturbed and unperturbed predictive distributions. Pixels whose predictions shift the most are the pixels the perturbation moved.

What the architecture needs

  • Distributional predictions — so we can take KL divergences.
  • Factorized latents — so local edits stay local.
  • Random-access decoding — so we can query any pixel cheaply.

LRAS has all three, and KL-tracing on LRAS is competitive with specialized flow models on standard benchmarks without any fine-tuning.
