# Robot Capabilities as Probabilistic Inference over Next Token Prediction

Published research post: https://neuroailab.github.io/psi-website/psiborg/

The page integrates the many-path prompting animation, the three studied capabilities, and the accompanying policy, simulation, and progress results. Main animation: 5443 frames at 30 fps, presented at 1.2× speed (151.19 seconds). Sources are in capability-animation; `node capability-animation/build.mjs` builds hero.js after its dependencies are installed.

The post opens in dark mode on each visit; the theme toggle changes the current view. Three compact resource buttons retain their symbols, Code/Paper/Model labels and SOON badges. The title, resource row, full 16:9 main player and controls fit the initial viewport.

Click/tap a video to pause or resume. The main film and result panels provide seek bars; the main film also supports Space/Enter and chapter navigation. Result thumbnails remain small while the selected video stays large. All media URLs are relative to this directory.

The publication omits the local editing-comments form and obsolete animation downloads. Failure detection remains inactive.

Downloads: [BibTeX](psiborg.bib) and the separate [20-second teaser](assets/capability-film/PsiBorg-teaser-v3.mp4). The former `/psibot/` article URL forwards here, preserving query strings and section anchors.
